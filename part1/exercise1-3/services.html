Thread & Art Tailoring

Thread & Art Tailoring is a tailoring shop in jabang,The Gambia. We create custom-made couture outfits, alter clothing, and help customers bring their fashion ideas to life.

Custom-Made Clothing

We design and sew outfits to suit each customer's style and measurements.

  • Traditional outfits
  • Modern fashion designs
  • Special occasion outfits

Clothing Alterations and Repairs

We adjust and repair cloths to improve their fit and appearance.

  • Clothing repairs
    • Replacing zips
    • Fixing torn clothes
    • Replacing buttons
  • Adjusting clothing sizes
  • Shortening trouser and dresses

How to Order Outfits

Follow these steps to have an outfit made just for you.

  1. Choose a design and fabric.
  2. Provide your measurements.
  3. Attend a fitting.
  4. Collect your finished outfit.
