How to start a Tailoring Business

This guide is for anyone who wants to start a small tailoring business and offer clothing services to customers.

Getting Started

Starting a tailoring business requires sewing skills, creativity and plan for attracting customers.

  • Choose the types of clothing you want to make.
  • Decide where you will work.
  • Plan how you will advertise your service.

Equipments and Materials

Prepare the basic tools you need to measure,cut and sew fabric

Essential Tools

  • Sewing machine
  • Measuring tape
  • Scissors and needles
  • Thread, fabric and pins

Serving Customer

A clear process helps understand how their cloths will be made.

  1. Discuss the customer's design and needs.
  2. Take accurate measurements
  3. Choose the fabric and agree on the price.
  4. Sew the garment and check the fit.
  5. Make any necessary adjustments and deliver the clothing.
