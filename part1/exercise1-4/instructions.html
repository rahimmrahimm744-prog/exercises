How to prepare Chicken Stew and Rice

This recipe is for anyone who want to prepare a delicious chicken stew rice at home.

What you need

  • Chicken
  • Rice
  • Cooking oil
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Salt and seasonings

Steps

  1. Wash the chicken and season it with salt and seasoning.
  2. Chop the onions and tomatoes.
  3. Heat the cooking oil in a pot and fry the onions.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook until they soften.
  5. Add the chicken and water, then cook until the chicken is tender.
  6. Wash the rice and cook it in a separate pot until soft.
  7. Serve the chicken stew with the rice.immediately.

If something goes wrong

If the stew is too salty, add a little water. be careful with hot oil because it can cause serious burns. If the chicken is still raw inside cook it for longer.
