How to Apply for a National ID Card

Getting the Application Form

The first step is to obtain the application form to begin the national ID card application process.

Providing Your Birth Certificate

Provide the requested detail from your birth certificate and summit a photocopy of the certificate

Answering Question About Yourself

During registration, answer the questions asked about yourself to provide the information required for your application.

Answering Questions

Go to the photo room to have your photo taken for your national ID card.

Having Your Photograph Taken

After answering the registration question, go to the photo room, where your photograph is taken for your national ID card.

Collecting Your National ID Card

After registration, you are given a date to return and collect yor national ID card when it is ready.
